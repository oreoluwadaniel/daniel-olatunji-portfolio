**Data Analyst | Business Intelligence Analyst | Lagos, Nigeria**

## How the case studies are presented

Each case study aims to answer:

1. What business problem needed to be understood?
2. Which decisions and business questions shaped the analysis?
3. What data and methods were used, and how were the results checked?
4. What did the evidence reveal?
5. Why do the findings matter to the business?
6. What should happen next?
7. What impact was measured, and what remains a projection or a recommendation?
8. What technical evidence is available for review?

Screenshots are included where verified report images are available. Project findings are distinguished from modelled scenarios and unmeasured future benefits. No recommendation is presented as a realised business result without evidence.
